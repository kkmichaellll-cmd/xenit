FINAL WORKFLOW
1. The tenant uploads their access-card photo and selects Print My Access Card. The print action is recorded and the tenant returns to the dashboard.
2. The admin opens Admin > Applications & payments and selects the tenant. In Access Card & HOA / Utility, the admin reviews the photo and enters:
   - HOA Move-In Fee
   - Electric (Xcel Energy)
   - Cable + Internet
   - Last Month's Rent
   - Payee name, purpose, mailing address, city, state and ZIP code
   - Four money-order instructions, Additional instructions and the USPS postal-money-order setting
3. The calculated total updates automatically. The admin saves the settings, then selects Approve access card & enable HOA & Utility.
4. The tenant receives an email and the HOA & Utility button appears on the tenant dashboard.
5. The tenant sees the move-in letter, itemized charges and calculated total. Click here to make your payment opens the internal Money Order or Cashier's Check page.
6. The tenant uploads a JPG, PNG or PDF receipt up to 20 MB. The admin email contains the receipt as an attachment.
7. While the payment proof is pending, the dashboard shows a disabled “Utility & HOA Under Review” button.
8. The admin downloads and reviews the payment proof, then selects Approve payment. The tenant receives an approval email and the dashboard shows “Validate Money Order.”
9. Validate Money Order opens the same mailing-receipt workflow used by the earlier payment stages. The tenant uploads a JPG, PNG or PDF mailing receipt up to 20 MB and may add a tracking number.
10. The admin receives the receipt by email, downloads it in the application workspace, and approves or declines it with an optional note. A declined receipt can be replaced; an approved receipt completes the validation step.

SECURITY AND DATA RULES
The server checks the signed-in tenant, approved application, final mailing-receipt approval, access-card record and admin release before revealing or accepting Utility & HOA payment details. Settings are locked after release so an issued total cannot be changed. The total is calculated from the four stored charges. Receipt downloads use short-lived signed URLs. Repeated print, release and approval actions do not create duplicate notifications.
